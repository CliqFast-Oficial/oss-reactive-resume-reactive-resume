The added skills use original instructions and examples, with the existing Reactive Resume skills supplying the product-specific boundaries. ## Specific ResumeSkills references

- [Academic CV builder](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/academic-cv-builder/SKILL.md): publications, teaching, funding and service need distinct treatment. The adapted skill preserves publication status, author order and grant attribution without prescribing one discipline's customs to everyone.
- [Executive resume writer](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/executive-resume-writer/SKILL.md): leadership scope and strategic decisions. The adapted skill separates company revenue, P&L, budget and reporting authority.
- [Career changer translator](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/career-changer-translator/SKILL.md): make transferable capabilities explicit. The adapted skill retains the original professional setting and never translates adjacent experience into a qualification the person lacks.
- [Tech resume optimizer](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/tech-resume-optimizer/SKILL.md): connect technical work to scope and results. Separate engineering, data and product guidance avoids treating their evidence as interchangeable.
- [Cover letter generator](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/cover-letter-generator/SKILL.md): connect evidence to an employer's needs. The adapted skill does not invent enthusiasm, referrals or research and has no universal “perfect” opening.
- [LinkedIn profile optimizer](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/linkedin-profile-optimizer/SKILL.md): profile fields serve a broader audience than a single application. The adapted skill separates public disclosure from private resume content and asks for current field limits when they matter.
- [Portfolio case study writer](https://github.com/Paramchoudhary/ResumeSkills/blob/74ae19e7c62b0516d1c298328e5544976c12da5d/skills/portfolio-case-study-writer/SKILL.md): explain problem, choices, contribution and results. The adapted skill distinguishes shipped work from prototypes and does not invent a research process or causal lift.

## Advice not carried forward

- Example numbers, credentials, dates or outcomes that were not present in the example's input. An example teaches structure; it never supplies candidate facts.
- Claims that public GitHub activity is required for engineering roles, or that a single length, section order or photo convention fits every applicant.
- Claimed recruiter attention windows, profile-view multipliers, universal keyword thresholds or ATS pass guarantees.
- Automatic equivalence between different professions' tools, authority or regulated qualifications.
- Promotion of pending, simulated or observed outcomes into awarded, deployed or causal results.

The role guides are editorial prompts, not authoritative hiring standards. Employer instructions, local requirements and the user's verified facts take precedence. Existing evidence references remain under the individual original skills for questions that require their deeper research.
